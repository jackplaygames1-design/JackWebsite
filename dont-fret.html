My role

Three years of building strange characters for a stranger world.

Don’t Fret is a defining long-term project in my work and the strongest example of how I approach character production for games.

My contributions span concept translation, design refinement, character modeling, high-poly sculpting, retopology, UV work, optimization, and rigging support. The goal is always the same: preserve what makes the design memorable while making it practical in real time.
