The short version

Artist brain.
Production mindset.

I started in Blender more than ten years ago as a Five Nights at Freddy’s accuracy modeler. That obsession with shape, proportion, and tiny details grew into a broader character-art practice.

Today I build stylized characters, creatures, and occasional environments. My favorite projects sit between playful and unsettling: expressive enough to remember, but grounded enough to work in a real game pipeline.

On Don’t Fret, I collaborated with concept artists to translate designs into 3D, refine forms for in-engine readability, sculpt higher-detail passes, and prepare optimized assets. That kind of cross-discipline problem solving is where I do my best work.

I care about a clean result, but I also care about how it gets there—clear topology, practical decisions, and a character that keeps its personality after the technical work is done.
